Privacy

Your files never leave your browser. The tools read them on your device. Each page's content policy lets it talk to Google Analytics and nothing else, so a file can't be sent anywhere.

We count visits with Google Analytics: the page, the site that sent you, your browser, screen size and rough location. It sets a cookie to tell a return visit from a new one. Google's own page says how Google uses this information.

If your device is set to a European time zone, we ask first, and nothing goes to Google until you say yes. Elsewhere visits are counted unless you turn it off below.

No ads, no ad tracking, and Google's signals for ads are off.

Your choice is kept in this browser.
